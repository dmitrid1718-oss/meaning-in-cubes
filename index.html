Seven-day observation experiment

Meaning in Cubes

Look briefly. Notice what appears before you try to explain it.

Each form is an original cube-built image designed to leave room for projection, association, and ambiguity.

Return once a day for seven consecutive days.
You may observe one form or all twenty. Completion is not the point. Continuity is.

For reflection only. The forms are not diagnostic and this is not a psychological or medical assessment.
